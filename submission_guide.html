    Complaint Submission Guide

    Guidelines, expected timelines, and procedures for filing academic and course-related complaints at Ambo University.

    Submission Steps

    1

    Informal Resolution First

    Whenever possible, attempt to discuss the issue informally with your course instructor during designated office hours before logging a formal complaint.

    2

    Gather Factual Evidence

    Prepare question numbers, exam dates, marked assignment rubrics, or official field trip permission letters to attach to your submission.

    3

    Fill and Submit the Online Form

    Choose the correct department and course, select the appropriate urgency level, write a clear objective statement, and submit.

    4

    Track Status Updates

    Check "My Complaints" periodically. Once the teacher provides a resolution and the department head confirms it, the case will be marked "Resolved".

    ✅ Valid Grounds for Complaint

    • Calculation or addition errors on final mark sheets.
    • Failure to announce continuous assessment results on time.
    • Inconsistent or unexplained attendance deductions.
    • Failure to deliver lectures according to announced course syllabus.
    • Lab equipment or workspace unavailability during scheduled practicals.

    ❌ Invalid Grounds / Out of Scope

    • Personal disagreements unrelated to course delivery.
    • Non-academic facility issues (dormitory, cafeteria, library terminal Wi-Fi).
    • Disagreements with legitimate academic grading judgment without evidence of rubric deviation.
    • Complaints submitted anonymously or on behalf of third parties.
