    Complaint Submission Guide

    Official guidelines, verifiable criteria, and expected service-level timelines for filing academic grievances at Ambo University.

    Submission Procedure

    1

    Informal Consultation First

    Whenever possible, consult informally with your course instructor during designated departmental office hours before submitting a formal grievance.

    2

    Gather Verifiable Evidence

    Prepare exam papers, continuous assessment rubrics, official course syllabi, or attendance logs to substantiate your claim.

    3

    File the Academic Complaint

    Select your enrolled department, course, urgency tier, and provide a clear, factual statement of the issue through the portal.

    4

    Track Triage & Formal Resolution

    Follow status updates on your student timeline. Once the instructor responds and the department head confirms the outcome, the case closes with formal documentation.

    Scope of Legitimate Academic Grievances

    Valid Grounds for Grievance

    • Calculation or addition discrepancies on midterm and final exam scores.
    • Failure to announce continuous assessment marks prior to final examinations.
    • Discrepancies in official attendance deductions without prior notification.
    • Substantial deviation from published course syllabi or lab schedules.
    • Inaccessible equipment or software resources during scheduled practical lab sessions.

    Out of Scope / Invalid Grounds

    • Personal disagreements unrelated to academic instruction and course delivery.
    • Campus facility maintenance (dormitory, cafeteria, non-academic facility requests).
    • Challenges to legitimate academic judgment without evidence of rubric deviation.
    • Anonymous filings or submissions submitted on behalf of third parties.
