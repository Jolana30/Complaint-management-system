    Statutory Committee Jurisdiction & Charter

    Under Ambo University Senate Legislation, the Disciplinary Committee exercises formal jurisdiction over complaints involving retaliatory grading threats, intimidation, academic integrity breaches, and ethical violations. Departmental mediation ceases once a complaint is formally escalated to this docket. All decisions are logged with a cryptographic audit signature.
