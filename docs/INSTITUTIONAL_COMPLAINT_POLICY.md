# Ambo University Academic Grievance & Complaint Management Policy
**Document Reference:** AU-SEN-POL-2026-04  
**Issuing Authority:** Office of the Academic Vice President (AVP) & Senate Academic Standards Committee  
**Target Audience:** Undergraduate & Postgraduate Students, Academic Faculty, Department Heads, College Deans  

---

## 1. Executive Mandate & Purpose
Ambo University is dedicated to academic excellence, instructional integrity, transparent grading, and equitable student-faculty engagement. This policy establishes the official institutional framework and procedural rules governing student academic grievances across all campuses (Awaro Main Campus, Achat Campus, Waliso Campus, and Guder Campus).

The **Ambo University Complaint Management System (Ambo CMS)** provides a auditable, digital mechanism to receive, triage, investigate, and resolve grievances fairly, systematically, and within defined Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

---

## 2. Institutional Escalation Hierarchy (5-Tier Framework)

Academic grievances must follow an orderly hierarchical progression. No case may skip a level without certified non-responsiveness (exceeding the maximum SLA):

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Level 1: Course Instructor (Informal / 1st Response)        │
│ SLA: 24 - 48 hours | Focus: Calculation, Rubric review      │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ (If unresolved / disputed)
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Level 2: Department Head & DAC (Academic Commission)        │
│ SLA: 3 - 5 business days | Focus: Independent re-marking     │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ (If unresolved / disputed)
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Level 3: College Dean & CAC (College Academic Commission)   │
│ SLA: 5 - 7 business days | Focus: Formal inquiry committee   │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ (Ethics / Systemic violations)
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Level 4: University Ethics Directorate & Ombudsperson       │
│ Focus: Whistleblower protection, harassment, misconduct     │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ (Final appellate review)
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Level 5: Academic Vice President (AVP) & Senate Committee   │
│ Final binding institutional determination                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Tier Descriptions:
1. **Level 1 (Course Instructor):**
   - The primary reviewer for instructional or grading questions.
   - Evaluates answer scripts, continuous assessment rubrics, and attendance sheets.
   - Proposes adjustments or provides reasoned written explanations.
2. **Level 2 (Department Head & Department Academic Commission - DAC):**
   - Reviews instructor findings and confirms case resolution.
   - Appoints two independent departmental faculty members for blind re-marking if score disputes persist.
   - Signs official mark modification vouchers forwarded to the Campus Registrar.
3. **Level 3 (College Dean & College Academic Commission - CAC):**
   - Handles multi-department or systemic grievances.
   - Convenes formal ad-hoc investigation committees for curriculum deviations.
4. **Level 4 (University Ethics Directorate & Student Ombudsperson):**
   - Independent statutory office protecting students against retaliation or intimidation.
   - Primary recipient of confidential whistleblowing and ethics complaints.
5. **Level 5 (Academic Vice President & Senate Academic Standards Committee):**
   - Supreme academic arbiter for final appeals. Decisions are permanently binding.

## 3. Protocol for Anonymous Complaints

A critical question in grievance administration is: **"How can a teacher resolve an issue without knowing who complained?"**

Ambo University establishes a dual-track classification:

```
                              ┌─────────────────────────┐
                              │ Student Files Grievance │
                              └────────────┬────────────┘
                                           │
                    Is it an individual course/grade correction?
                                    /             \
                                  YES              NO (Ethics, Harassment,
                                   /                Retaliation, Bias)
                                  /                   \
                                 ▼                     ▼
          ┌──────────────────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────────────────┐
          │ IDENTIFIED TRACK             │   │ CONFIDENTIAL ANONYMOUS TRACK │
          ├──────────────────────────────┤   ├──────────────────────────────┤
          │ • Student ID mandatory       │   │ • Identity hidden/anonymized │
          │ • Course lecturer assigned   │   │ • Bypasses course teacher    │
          │ • Grade voucher modified     │   │ • Routed to Dept Head / Dean │
          │ • Registrar ledger updated   │   │ • Handled by Ombudsperson    │
          └──────────────────────────────┘   └──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Track A: Identified Academic Grievances (Mandatory Identity)
- **Scope:** Grade disputes, exam score additions, continuous assessment calculation, attendance deduction adjustments, makeup exam eligibility.
- **Rule:** A teacher cannot modify an individual student's official grade or exam marks without the student's verified identification number (`ugr/xxxxx/xx`). The Campus Registrar legally requires verified student credentials on official mark alteration vouchers.
- **Protection:** Instructors are strictly bound by the Ambo University Code of Conduct; retaliatory grading or punitive behavior against a complainant is an immediate dismissible offense investigated by the Ethics Directorate.

### Track B: Confidential / Anonymous Grievances (Teacher Bypassed)
- **Scope:** Professional misconduct, chronic lecturer absenteeism, sexual harassment, ethnic or religious bias, bribery/extortion, or systemic unfairness.
- **Rule:** Anonymous filings **strictly bypass the classroom instructor** and route directly to the **Department Head, College Dean, and Student Ombudsperson**.
- **Investigation:** The Department Head conducts an objective departmental audit (e.g., inspecting full class grade distributions, reviewing classroom attendance logs, interviewing student representatives collectively) without revealing any individual identity.

## 4. Legitimate vs Out-of-Scope Grounds

### 4.1 Admissible Academic Grievances
- Calculation, addition, or recording discrepancies on midterm/final examination papers.
- Failure of an instructor to disclose continuous assessment marks (minimum 50%) prior to final examinations as mandated by Senate legislation.
- Discrepancies in official attendance deductions without prior documented warning.
- Substantial, undocumented deviation from the approved course syllabus or laboratory schedule.
- Inaccessible equipment, software licenses, or facilities during scheduled practical sessions.

### 4.2 Non-Admissible Inquiries
- Challenges solely against legitimate academic judgment where rubrics were uniformly applied.
- Complaints regarding dormitory, cafeteria, clinic, or non-academic campus facilities (routed to Student Affairs Directorate).
- Malicious filings made with fabricated evidence.

## 5. Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

| Urgency Level | Criteria | Initial Triage | Faculty Investigation | Final Closure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical** | Grade submission deadlines, graduation eligibility, disciplinary suspension | Within 12 hrs | Within 24 hrs | **24 – 36 hrs** |
| **High** | End-of-semester course score disputes, prerequisite registration blocks | Within 24 hrs | Within 48 hrs | **2 – 3 business days** |
| **Medium** | Midterm exam calculations, lab schedule changes, attendance deductions | Within 48 hrs | Within 4 business days | **5 business days** |
| **Low** | General instructional feedback, syllabus pacing, reference textbook queries | Within 72 hrs | Within 7 business days | **7 – 10 business days** |
