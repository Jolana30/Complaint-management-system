Complaint Submission Guide

Follow these steps before sending a complaint to the department office.

How to Submit a Complaint

  1. 1

    Prepare your information

    Write your full name, student ID, department, course, and the main issue you want to report.

  2. 2

    Choose the correct complaint type

    Select the category that best matches your issue, such as grading, attendance, communication, or teaching quality.

  3. 3

    Explain the complaint clearly

    Use polite language and describe what happened, when it happened, and what kind of support you need.

  4. 4

    Attach supporting files

    If you have proof, such as marks, screenshots, notices, or documents, attach them before submitting.

  5. 5

    Track your complaint

    After submission, check your student dashboard for the complaint status and any replies from the responsible teacher.

Submit only real complaints related to academic or department issues. False or disrespectful complaints may be rejected by the administrator.
