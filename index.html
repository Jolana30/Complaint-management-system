    Complaint Resolution Process

    4-Stage Workflow
    1

    Submit Grievance

    Student selects department, course, urgency, and provides clear evidence.

    2

    Admin Assignment

    Admin verifies complaint validity and assigns it to the designated course teacher.

    3

    Teacher Response

    Teacher investigates, formulates a corrective action or clarification, and replies.

    4

    Confirmation & Closure

    Admin confirms resolution; student is notified with official outcome in their dashboard.
