    Ambo University Complaint Management System

    A transparent, structured, and auditable academic grievance platform ensuring fair review of grading, assessment, and departmental matters between students and faculty.

    Login to Portal Read Submission Guide

    Platform Core Capabilities

    Verifiable Audit Trail

    Every grievance generates an immutable chronological timeline documenting submission, administrative delegation, teacher feedback, and final sign-off.

    Departmental Oversight

    Academic department heads inspect all complaints, verify course contexts, and review teacher replies before certifying official case closure.

    Fair Academic Recourse

    Structured dispute resolution for mid-term assessments, lab scoring, lecture communication, and attendance records directly with course lecturers.

    Academic Grievance Resolution Workflow

    Standard University Operating Procedure
    1 Student

    Lodge Grievance

    Select department and course, specify category and urgency level, and provide supporting academic evidence.

    2 Admin

    Review & Assign

    Department administrator validates grievance admissibility and assigns case to the designated instructor.

    3 Faculty

    Teacher Investigation

    Course instructor reviews submission, re-evaluates assessment materials, and drafts official corrective findings.

    4 Admin

    Confirmation & Closure

    Department head confirms resolution, updates student academic ledger, and issues final determination notice.
