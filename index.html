    Senate Anti-Retaliation Protection: Submitting a documented assessment dispute is an official student right. All grade adjustments require Department Head confirmation and are permanently sealed in the audit registry.

    The 3 Steps to Complaint Resolution

    Every case moves through three auditable checkpoints to ensure fairness and procedural integrity:

    1

    Submission & Evidence Review

    Student registers the course code, assessment type, and original marked quiz or exam script showing the arithmetic or rubric discrepancy.

    2

    Independent Academic Review

    Course lecturer evaluates the script against the published answer key. Serious misconduct bypasses the department directly to the Senate Disciplinary Committee.

    3

    Department Confirmation & Registrar Update

    Department Head verifies the resolution and certifies any grade amendment before submitting it to the University Registrar.
